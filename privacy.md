# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-10_

Personalized Content Studio ("the app") is a personal, open-source tool operated by Krish. It lets a creator publish their own videos and captions to their own YouTube, Facebook Page and Instagram professional accounts.

## What the app accesses
When you log in with Facebook, you grant the app only the permissions you approve:
- your list of Facebook Pages and basic Page information (`pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`)
- permission to publish posts to a Page you manage (`pages_manage_posts`)
- your linked Instagram professional account and permission to publish Reels to it (`instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`)

The app uses this access only to publish the videos and captions that you choose, when you ask it to.

## What is stored, and where
- Access tokens are stored on your own device, in your operating system's secure credential store.
- A history of your publish attempts (titles, captions, status, links) is stored in a local database file on your own device.
- Your videos are read from your device and sent directly to the platform you selected.

The app has no server of its own. I do not receive, collect, store, sell or share your data, and the app contains no analytics or advertising.

## Third parties
Data you publish is sent to Meta (Facebook, Instagram) or Google (YouTube), and is then governed by their privacy policies.

## Your choices and deletion
You can disconnect at any time and delete all data the app holds. See [Data Deletion Instructions](data-deletion).

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
