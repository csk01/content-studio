# Data Deletion Instructions

Personalized Content Studio keeps your data only on your own device. I hold no copy of it. To delete everything:

1. **Remove the stored login:** run `studio logout facebook` (and `studio logout instagram`) in the app. This deletes the access token from your device.
2. **Remove the app's access at Facebook:** go to Facebook **Settings → Business Integrations** (or **Apps and Websites**), find the app, and choose **Remove**. For Instagram, also check **Settings → Apps and Websites** in the Instagram app.
3. **Delete the local history:** delete the folder `.studio` in your user directory (for example `C:\Users\<you>\.studio`).
4. **Posts already published** to Facebook or Instagram are not controlled by the app. Delete them directly on those platforms.
